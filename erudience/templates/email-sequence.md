# Email sequence template

This is a structure, not reusable copy. Every bracketed field requires source-backed content.

## Email 1

Subject: [Specific capability or operational gap]

Hi [first name],

[One current, specific observation about the company.]

[Explain the practical gap or opportunity implied by that observation.]

[Propose one concrete Erudience use case and how it would fit the existing telephony or client relationship.]

[Ask one low-friction, relevant question.]

Best,

Bishal Paul

[approved sender]

[approved regional opt-out language]

## Follow-up rules

- Disabled until approved in run control.
- Do not send after a reply, bounce, opt-out, or complaint.
- Every follow-up must add relevant value or context.
- Never use fake urgency, false familiarity, or a fabricated resource.
