# Discovery, demos, proposals and handover

## Discovery record

Capture the partner's customers, repeat problem, current workflow, monthly volume if supplied, present costs if supplied, systems, integration access, ownership, urgency, decision process and agreed next action. Ask the most useful unanswered questions, not a long interrogation. Do not promise savings, production access or delivery dates before scoping.

## Objections

| Objection | Useful response direction |
| --- | --- |
| We already provide AI | Ask where delivery or integration capacity is missing; close if no complementary fit |
| Our clients prefer people | Explore routine-call support with human escalation; do not assert full replacement |
| We lack demand | Ask for a concrete customer workflow; avoid pushing a generic demo |
| What does it cost? | Establish scope and usage; use the owner-approved rate card or request pricing approval |
| Reliability concerns | Explain test criteria, fallback and monitoring only where demonstrable |
| Who owns the customer? | Surface account, data and support responsibilities for a written agreement |

## Demo brief

State audience, scenario, fictional data, disclosure, conversation flow, integration mock boundaries, success cases, fallback cases and limitations. Label demonstration audio as AI-generated. Never imply a synthetic conversation is a real customer call or that a disconnected integration is live.

## Proposal checklist

Include business objective, scope, deliverables, integration boundaries, responsibilities, dependencies, test/acceptance criteria, timeline assumptions, data/security requirements, usage limits, support, escalation and approved fees. Keep white-label/referral/reseller terms explicit and owner-approved. Request legal review where needed rather than inventing terms.

## Handover

Require accepted proposal/contract evidence, commercial owner sign-off, implementation owner, client contacts in private CRM, agreed requirements, success criteria, credentials via secure channel, billing trigger and kickoff date. Sales promises become a tracked delivery checklist. Close lost with reason and next permission state; do not erase history.
