# Refinement and activation roadmap

## Phase 1: make the existing pipeline trustworthy

Choose the private source of truth; reconcile the saved 42-row dashboard with the reported 47-row version; retain exclusions and provenance. Import with stable IDs and re-evaluate contradictory statuses. Revalidate a five-lead shortlist. Exit: five complete profiles with missing fields explicit, no duplicates and no false ready labels.

## Phase 2: sharpen the offer

Approve one target segment and one partner proposition. Decide referral/co-sell/white-label model, account ownership, support responsibilities, pricing authority and permitted proof. Prepare a specific demo with fictional data. Exit: reviewed offer brief and owner-approved claims; no invented margins or case studies.

## Phase 3: connect and test

Connect Apify only if useful; test five domains with an explicit cap. Select email verification. Confirm exact sender and private CRM, then test read, draft, synthetic CRM writes and an authorised owner-recipient email. Exit: documented real IDs, correct data mapping and verified provider results. No n8n dependency is required.

## Phase 4: supervised sales pilot

Prepare five personalised messages, review sources and sending gates, obtain exact approvals and send within the approved lane. Track human replies, meetings and objections. Exit: reconciled activity history, working suppression and a concrete learning report.

## Phase 5: durable automation

Implement private CRM persistence, transactional action reservation, provider events, reply/opt-out processing, scheduler, monitoring, pause control and recovery. Test duplicate delivery, ambiguous timeout, expired token, changed approval, hard bounce and opt-out before unattended activation. Exit: no duplicate sends in replay tests and all unresolved actions held safely.

## Phase 6: improve conversion and scale

Use real replies to refine the ICP, offer, demo and proposal. Add approved pricing, partner onboarding and won-deal handover. Expand one segment or market at a time when qualified conversations justify it. Set explicit budget and volume caps. Measure opportunities and revenue rather than lead volume alone.

## Owner decisions needed for activation

Exact sender mailbox; private CRM location; confirmed partner offer/model; approved demo/proof; verification provider; Apify spend cap if used; initial sending cap and campaign requirements. These decisions do not block maintaining the draft system.

Start with the Australia telecom plan. Newsletter/nurture/reactivation require real business-scoped relationship evidence before activation. Coordinate work through governance/COLLABORATION.md and the work board.
