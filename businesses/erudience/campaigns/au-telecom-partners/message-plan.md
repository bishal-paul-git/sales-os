# Australia telecom partner message plan

Campaign: `CAMPAIGN-AU-TELECOM-2026`. Status: structural draft. These are message components, not approved send payloads. Every recipient requires current evidence, a completed private personalisation brief and exact owner approval.

## Core message

Erudience can help a telecom or managed-service partner explore one scoped AI call-handling and connected follow-up workflow alongside its existing service. The first step is to confirm whether the problem is relevant and who owns the area.

## Segment angles

| Segment | Problem angle | Pilot angle |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Telecom consultant or channel adviser | A client has call handling or follow-up needs outside the partner's current delivery scope | Bring in specialist discovery and implementation for one customer workflow |
| VoIP, PBX or UCaaS provider | The phone platform connects calls but the customer still has missed, overflow or repetitive-call work | Add one bounded conversational workflow without claiming to replace the phone service |
| Phone-system specialist | Customers ask for after-hours capture, routing or structured follow-up | Test a narrow workflow with explicit transfer and fallback rules |
| Communications-focused MSP | Call outcomes remain disconnected from tasks, follow-up or approved business systems | Map one call-to-action path after confirming access and data boundaries |

## Personalisation rule

Use one observation that is demonstrably current and relevant, such as a named business telephony service, partner programme, customer segment or supported communications platform. Save the source URL and check date privately. Do not infer demand, scale, customers or strategic priorities that the source does not establish.

## Email 1: relevance

Subject options:

- A possible extension to [company]'s [service]
- Question about [company]'s customer call workflows

Structure:

1. State the verified observation.
2. Connect it to one plausible call-handling or follow-up problem.
3. Explain the one-workflow partner pilot in one sentence.
4. Ask whether this is relevant or who owns the area.

Working draft:

> Hi [first name],
>
> I saw that [company] [verified observation]. Some providers in that position encounter customers who want help with [one relevant problem] beyond the core phone service.
>
> Erudience works on voice AI and connected automation. We are exploring a partner pilot around one scoped workflow, with clear routing and human fallback, rather than a broad platform change.
>
> Is that relevant to your customer work, or is there someone else who owns this area?
>
> Bishal

The phrase "some providers" is contextual, not a performance claim. Replace it if a sourced customer signal provides a more specific opening.

## Email 2: useful detail

Send only if the recipient remains eligible and the first message has no human reply.

> Hi [first name],
>
> One example of the scope I had in mind is [one workflow, such as overflow call capture with an agreed escalation path]. The first step would be to map the intents, handoff and systems before suggesting an implementation.
>
> If that comes up in [company]'s customer work, I can send a short outline of the pilot questions.
>
> Bishal

Do not claim the example is already deployed for Erudience unless approved proof exists.

## Email 3: close the loop

Send only if the recipient remains eligible and there has been no human reply.

> Hi [first name],
>
> I will close the loop after this. If AI call handling or connected follow-up becomes relevant for one of [company]'s customers, I would be glad to compare notes on a narrowly scoped pilot.
>
> If this is not useful, no reply is needed and I will not continue the sequence.
>
> Bishal

Apply the approved opt-out wording and headers for the selected sender and campaign requirements. The sentence above does not replace a working suppression process.

## Reply routing

| Reply intent | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Interested | Pause automation; qualify the use case and agree a discovery step |
| Referral | Pause automation; record the referral and establish the new contact basis before writing |
| Not now | Record timing and permission; do not create an arbitrary follow-up date |
| Not relevant | Close or requalify the account; do not argue |
| Unsubscribe or complaint | Suppress immediately across the applicable Erudience scope |
| Technical or commercial question | Answer only from approved facts; escalate unapproved pricing, terms, integration or proof |
