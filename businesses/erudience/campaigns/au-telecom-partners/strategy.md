# Australia telecom partner pilot

Campaign ID: `CAMPAIGN-AU-TELECOM-2026`. Business: Erudience. Type: `partner-outreach`. Status: `validation`. All sending is disabled.

## Objective

Validate whether a small number of Australian telecom and managed-service partners will discuss one customer call-handling or follow-up workflow with Erudience. The first learning batch is five existing high-fit candidates, not a new bulk list.

The campaign is successful when it produces evidence about partner demand, objections, decision ownership and a useful next step. A positive reply is not proof of revenue, and an unanswered email is not proof the offer has no market.

## Campaign hypothesis

A partner is more likely to engage when the message:

- starts from a customer problem adjacent to its existing service;
- offers a bounded discovery and pilot rather than a broad transformation;
- leaves referral, co-sell, reseller and white-label structures open for discovery; and
- makes one low-friction request about relevance or decision ownership.

## Priority segments and angles

| Segment | Evidence to confirm | Working angle |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Telecom consultant or channel adviser | Current advice, procurement or account relationship with Australian businesses | Add a specialist call-handling and follow-up option when a client has a suitable need |
| VoIP, PBX or UCaaS provider | Current business calling, cloud telephony or phone-system offer | Extend the phone service into one scoped conversational workflow with clear human fallback |
| Phone-system specialist or installer | Installation, support and ongoing customer relationship | Address missed, overflow or after-hours calls without replacing the core phone service |
| MSP with a communications practice | Managed IT plus telephony, communications or customer-workflow responsibility | Connect call outcomes to follow-up or operational systems after technical discovery |

Do not infer customer demand from a category label alone. Each recipient needs current source evidence and a specific partner angle.

## Audience gates

Apply the shared partner qualification workflow. Before a draft can become sendable, confirm:

1. Australian customer relevance and current complementary service evidence.
2. Active legal entity and correct business identity.
3. One feasible registered use case for the partner or its customers.
4. Named owner of the partnership, commercial or service-portfolio decision.
5. No unresolved direct competitor conflict.
6. Appropriate business contact basis, current technical email verdict and suppression result.
7. Exact sender, content revision, recipient and action approval.

Unknowns earn no fit points and block sending. Preserve the source and checked date for every gate. Historic spreadsheet labels are not current evidence.

## Offer

Use `voice-automation-partner-pilot`: a jointly scoped AI call-handling and connected follow-up pilot beside the partner's existing service. Begin with one internal or end-customer workflow, defined intents, explicit escalation, approved knowledge, test data and acceptance criteria.

Possible workflows are AI reception and routing, lead follow-up, and appointment-request capture. Never imply that calendar booking, CRM updates, telephony compatibility or production deployment already works for a prospect. Those require discovery and testing.

Pricing, margins, commissions, exclusivity, delivery dates, guarantees and transferable proof remain unapproved. Do not use AIUK work as Erudience evidence.

## Message strategy

Use the campaign-specific [message plan](message-plan.md). Each email should contain:

- one current, source-backed observation about the partner;
- one relevant customer problem or workflow;
- one sentence explaining the bounded Erudience pilot; and
- one small CTA asking about relevance or the correct decision owner.

Avoid generic compliments, fabricated familiarity, inflated AI claims and long capability lists. Do not lead with a partner model, commercial incentive or calendar link.

## Sequence

The three proposed steps remain subject to owner approval:

1. Initial relevance question with one segment-specific angle.
2. Four business days later, add a concrete workflow example or clarify the pilot boundary.
3. Seven business days later, close the loop with no pressure and preserve the opt-out.

Stop on a human reply, unsubscribe, complaint, hard bounce or suppression. A referral reply pauses the sequence and creates a new qualification task; it does not authorise contact with the referred person automatically.

## Measurement and learning

Use delivered unique eligible recipients as the response denominator. Record human replies, positive or referral replies, accepted discovery steps, objections, bounces, complaints and suppressions. Treat opens as directional only.

After the five-record batch, review:

- which segment and problem framing earned a relevant response;
- whether the recipient owned the decision or referred it;
- what delivery, proof or commercial questions appeared;
- whether the proposed pilot felt sufficiently narrow; and
- which exclusions or verification failures protected the campaign.

Do not set conversion claims or scale thresholds until the observation window and owner decision are recorded.

## Activation path

1. Reconcile and connect the private five-record audience.
2. Recompute qualification and confirm the decision owner for each record.
3. Test the verification provider and record its verdict semantics and timestamps.
4. Confirm and test the Erudience sender, reply path and suppression process with an owner-controlled recipient.
5. Produce the five exact personalised drafts from the approved message plan.
6. Obtain recorded approval for the offer claims, cadence, recipients, sender and content revisions.
7. Run a supervised pilot within an approved cap, then reconcile provider events before any follow-up.

No campaign file, provider connection or draft grants permission to send.
