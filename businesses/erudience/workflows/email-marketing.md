# Erudience email marketing overlay

Follow core/workflows/email-marketing.md from the repository root. Apply core/policies/email-eligibility.md and this business's run control.

1. Resolve a campaign from campaigns/index.json. Start with the existing Australia telecom validation campaign unless the owner selects another.
2. Campaign Owner supplies target and objective; Offer Advisor selects one service/use case and records unapproved terms; Proof Steward validates business claims and AI disclosure for demos.
3. Shared roles research, qualify, draft and review using that context. Finish incomplete existing prospects before adding a large new batch. Never treat old spreadsheet flags as current verification.
4. Maintain the actual audience, approval history, suppressions and follow-ups in private Erudience storage. Current sender, CRM and paid verification remain unresolved.
5. Prepare an exact five-message approval batch only after contact evidence is sufficient. Do not send merely because a strategy or sequence exists.
6. Record observations and next tasks in the private campaign log, then update only public-safe decisions and blockers in memory.

Newsletter/nurture use subscription or enquiry records belonging to Erudience. Reactivation requires genuine prior relationships and a permitted recontact basis; do not import contacts belonging to another client or business.
