# AI call handling and follow-up partner pilot

Offer ID: `voice-automation-partner-pilot`. Status: `validation`. This is a working offer for the Australia telecom partner campaign. It is not an approved price, delivery promise or partner agreement.

## Who it is for

Australian telecom consultants, VoIP/PBX and UCaaS providers, phone-system specialists and MSPs that already advise or support business customers. The relevant decision owner normally controls partnerships, commercial services or the customer solution portfolio.

## Partner problem

A partner may see customer demand around missed calls, after-hours handling, repetitive enquiries, lead response and disconnected follow-up, but may not want to design and operate the full conversational workflow itself.

## Working proposition

Erudience helps the partner scope one narrow AI call-handling and connected follow-up workflow alongside the partner's existing phone or managed service. The starting point is a bounded pilot, not a platform-wide transformation.

The pilot can explore:

- answering an agreed set of routine call intents;
- collecting and structuring caller details;
- routing or escalating to the correct human path;
- capturing appointment requests without claiming a booking until the connected calendar confirms it;
- preparing follow-up tasks or CRM updates after a successful integration test; and
- producing structured outcomes for the partner and customer to review.

Every capability remains subject to technical discovery, approved knowledge, telephony compatibility, integration access, fallback design and acceptance testing.

## Pilot shape

1. Select one partner-owned internal workflow or one suitable end-customer workflow.
2. Map call intents, boundaries, escalation, data capture, follow-up and failure paths.
3. Confirm systems, permissions, disclosure needs and measurable acceptance criteria.
4. Build or configure only the agreed scope using fictional or owner-approved test data first.
5. Run supervised tests, record defects and review whether a production proposal is justified.

The campaign may describe this shape. It must not promise a specific implementation, integration, launch date or result before discovery.

## Partner model

Referral, co-sell, reseller and white-label are discovery options. Select only after discussing account ownership, proposal responsibility, branding, disclosure, support, data handling, pricing, liability and attribution. No margin, commission, exclusivity or recurring-revenue claim is approved.

## Qualification gates

A suitable partner has:

- evidence of a relevant Australian business-customer base;
- a complementary telecom, communications or managed-service offer;
- a plausible customer workflow matching at least one registered use case;
- a named owner for the partnership or commercial decision; and
- no unresolved direct-conflict, identity or suppression issue.

Hold the opportunity when the company mainly sells a competing AI implementation, wants unsupported guarantees, has no identifiable customer fit, or cannot establish an appropriate contact basis.

## Allowed campaign claims

- Erudience works on voice AI and connected automation.
- The proposed starting point is one scoped pilot.
- The workflow can be explored alongside the partner's current service.
- Referral, co-sell, reseller or white-label may be discussed in discovery.

## Claims that remain blocked

- prices, margins, commissions, savings or revenue outcomes;
- guaranteed delivery dates, uptime, accuracy or conversion results;
- named-customer results or AIUK work presented as Erudience evidence;
- a specific production integration before technical discovery; and
- a final partner model or division of responsibilities.

## Discovery CTA

Ask whether the partner is seeing relevant customer demand and who owns this area. If there is fit, propose a short discovery conversation around one customer workflow. Do not lead with a broad demo or an unapproved commercial incentive.
