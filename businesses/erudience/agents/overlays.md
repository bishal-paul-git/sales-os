# Erudience agent overlays

These roles supply business expertise to the shared agents, rather than duplicate their procedures.

| Business role | Context and decisions | Shared role it works with |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Campaign Owner | Select Erudience audience, campaign hypothesis, CTA and learning objective; maintain the registry | Sales Manager and Pipeline Manager |
| Offer Advisor | Match voice/automation service to partner/customer needs; clarify delivery assumptions and request price/partner-term approval | Researcher, Writer and Deal Advisor |
| Proof Steward | Check claim attribution, current website facts, demo boundaries and business voice; block unapproved AIUK attribution | Qualifier, Writer and Sales Manager |

Inputs are this business's knowledge, offer catalogue, campaign strategy and private relationship context. Output a campaign-scoped brief, offer review or claim decision with source/date. Never reuse another business's audience or permission record. Human commercial approval remains with Bishal unless explicitly delegated.
