# Business workspaces

Each business has its own knowledge, offers, approval rules, specialised agents, workflows, memory and campaign registry. Shared operating procedure is inherited from core/. Do not copy the core agent instructions into business folders.

Registered business: [Erudience](erudience/README.md). To add another business, use [_template](_template/README.md) and register it in registry.json. Do not assume the owner's other companies are authorised campaign senders here.
