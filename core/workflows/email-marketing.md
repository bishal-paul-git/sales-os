# Email marketing workflow

1. Resolve business, campaign and type. Load approved facts, offer, claims, audience basis and run control. Check the current private history before proposing new messages.
2. Plan the campaign objective, target audience, exclusions, value, CTA, sequence and measures. Use campaign strategy and data contract; unknown pricing or proof stays pending.
3. Prepare the audience. Research cold business contacts only for prospecting campaigns. For newsletter/nurture, validate the actual subscription/enquiry records and preferences instead of treating subscribers as partner prospects.
4. Validate sources, scope, contact route, exclusions and applicable suppression. Apply the campaign class eligibility rules. Deduplicate within a business and avoid overlapping sequences for the same recipient.
5. Draft a specific message/sequence with one clear objective and truthful personalisation. Newsletter copy must deliver the promised value. Never fabricate a resource, client result, relationship or urgency. Follow-ups follow campaign triggers, not a universal fixed cadence.
6. Review identity, tone, proof, links, opt-out process, recipient basis, exact sender, payload revision, schedule and landing experience. Test rendering/link routing with synthetic or owner-controlled data.
7. Obtain the applicable recorded owner approval. Provider availability is not approval. If any execution gate fails, leave a reviewed draft with the concrete blocker.
8. Send only via the confirmed business account within approved caps. Reserve the idempotency key, execute once and reconcile the provider result. A timeout is unknown until checked.
9. Process delivery, bounces, complaints, opt-outs and replies before more sends. Pause sequences on human replies for intent review. Suppress when required and propagate applicable scope across this business's campaigns.
10. Measure delivered contacts, human replies, useful clicks, positive replies and qualified next actions with explicit denominators. Treat opens as directional. Persist private events and write a public-safe learning summary.

For partner campaigns use qualification.md, messaging.md and sales-playbook.md as additional procedures. For deal-stage work use deals.md. A newsletter or nurture campaign does not require an ABN or partner score for every subscriber.
