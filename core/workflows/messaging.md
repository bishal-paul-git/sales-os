# Messaging and follow-up

## Initial message framework

Subject: a specific partnership topic, without deceptive Re:/Fwd: prefixes.

Hi [confirmed name],

[One factual observation about their relevant service or partner programme.]

[A concrete customer workflow the selected business could help with, framed as a proposal rather than an existing proven result.] [Explain how it complements the partner's role.]

[One question asking whether this fits their clients or who owns this area.]

[Approved sender identity, signature and required opt-out wording.]

This is a framework, not a ready-to-send template. Replace every placeholder and validate every claim.

## Synthetic example

Hi Jamie,

I saw Example Telecom offers hosted PBX to local service businesses. Would an AI reception option be useful for clients who need help handling routine calls outside office hours?

the selected business could scope the call handling and follow-up workflows alongside your phone service, with responsibilities agreed before a pilot.

Is this something you evaluate, or does a colleague look after new partner services?

The company and person above are fictional. Do not send this as a real prospect message.

## Follow-up defaults for owner review

Draft follow-up one after 4 business days and follow-up two after a further 7 business days, using the recipient's timezone/holidays. These are proposed intervals, not an active schedule. Add a relevant demo concept or one useful question, not "just checking in". Do not attach unsolicited large files.

Stop pending follow-ups on any reply and review intent. Unsubscribe, complaint, hard bounce or suppression stops the sequence. Not interested closes this campaign; do not automatically recycle into another. A later request becomes a dated task, not automatic permission for a new campaign.

## Quality review

Require evidence for personalisation; distinguish the partner's offer from the selected business's capabilities; use one CTA; remove unsupported numbers and named-client claims; verify links and recipient; check prior relationship; reject placeholders and fake familiarity. Record reviewer result and exact draft revision.
