# Email audience eligibility

| Campaign class | Audience evidence | Extra checks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Partner or direct prospecting | Named relevant business contact and owner-reviewed market/contact basis | Current service/entity evidence where campaign requires it, technical mailbox check, fit, conflict review |
| Newsletter or nurture | Subscription/permission record tied to this business, purpose and source | Topic expectations, preference state, suppression, valid contact route |
| Reactivation | Recorded prior relationship plus a permitted recontact basis | Last contact, reason for silence, permission freshness and previous objections |
| Demo follow-up | Actual enquiry/demo or agreed follow-up | Requested scope, agreed next step and recipient identity |
| Customer expansion | Actual business customer relationship and appropriate marketing basis | Account ownership, relevant service and existing communication preferences |

An email found online, a purchased list, a prior purchase, or an old conversation does not automatically satisfy the required contact basis. Legal requirements differ by region; campaign activation requires owner review of current applicable requirements. This is an operational gate, not legal advice.

All classes require correct business sender, audience eligibility, suppression, reviewed claims/links, approved payload or bounded lane, volume/spend limits, duplicate prevention and tested provider capability. Keep transactional messages separate from promotional campaigns. Unsubscribe/complaint stops promotional sequences; human replies pause automation for intent review. Hard bounces suppress the failed route. Unknown/catch-all verification does not become deliverable by assumption.

For newsletters and bulk promotional mail, verify a working opt-out/preference process and provider support for required headers/configuration before activation. Rate/cadence defaults are proposals until approved. Never move a subscriber into cold outreach to evade their preference state.
