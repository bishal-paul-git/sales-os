# Proposal checklist

- Business objective is explicit.
- Scope and exclusions are explicit.
- Deliverables and acceptance criteria are testable.
- Telephony, CRM, data and workflow integration boundaries are clear.
- Partner, the selected business and end-customer responsibilities are separated.
- Dependencies and assumptions are listed.
- Demo features are not represented as live production integrations.
- Timeline is based on approved assumptions.
- Data handling, retention, access and security requirements are stated.
- Usage limits, support, monitoring and escalation are defined.
- Pricing and commercial terms use the owner-approved revision.
- Referral, co-sell, reseller or white-label terms are explicit where relevant.
- Account ownership, branding, disclosure and liability need legal review where applicable.
- No guarantee, ROI, commission, discount, proof or client name is invented.
- Proposal revision, approver and approval date are recorded.
- Accepted promises are ready to become delivery handover items.
